## The coupon rules Vistaprint actually enforces

Vistaprint allows exactly one promo code per checkout, and cart-wide offers are capped at $100 in savings per order. Those two rules explain most "why didn't this work" moments, and almost no deal-list article spells them out.

The rest of the official fine print, from Vistaprint's offers page:

- Unless stated otherwise, codes don't apply to **delivery, taxes, design services, or digital products**
- Free-item and BOGO offers only cover products of **equal or lesser value**
- Percentage discounts can vary by product options, materials, finishes, colors, and quantity
- Cart-wide offers are capped at that **$100 maximum savings** figure

What *does* stack: shipping offers are applied after other discounts, US orders of **$100 or more** (pre-tax, after discounts, excluding Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico) get free economy shipping, and Vistaprint's military discount for active-duty and veteran customers is permanent and combines with most other offers. So a $95 order with a 20% code lands under the free-shipping line — sometimes adding one small item and keeping the code is cheaper than paying economy shipping on the smaller cart.

One more official quirk: you can't add a code after placing an order. Vistaprint's FAQ says your workaround is contacting customer service, who may credit your account if the order qualifies. Don't count on it — apply the code before you pay.

## FAQ

**Can I use two vistaprint coupon codes on one order?**
No. Vistaprint allows one promo code per checkout, so run the math and use whichever single code saves more. Cart-wide offers are additionally capped at $100 in savings per order.

**Can I add a promo code after placing my order?**
Not on your own. Vistaprint's official FAQ says to contact customer service; if the order qualifies, they may issue a refund or account credit. There's no self-serve option.

**Does cashback stack with Vistaprint promo codes?**
Only sometimes. Codes advertised on the cashback portal's own Vistaprint page are safe to combine. Codes from other sources — including the newsletter signup code — may make the cashback ineligible under the portal's published terms. If the code saves more than the cashback would, the code usually wins anyway.

The rate shown at click-through on your own portal's page is the one that applies.
